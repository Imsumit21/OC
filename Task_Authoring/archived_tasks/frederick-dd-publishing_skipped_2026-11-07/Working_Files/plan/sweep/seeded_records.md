DD publishing model: ground rules
Written for next Monday's talk. Leonard, Sep 29.

Who's in
Every publisher on Fred's outreach sheet, plus any publisher that came to one of the four of us about Domino Delights since Apr 1, 2025, however it reached us. A name someone passed along doesn't count unless they came to us. Co-dev, engine or investment talks aren't part of this, even where DD came up. That covers Homa and NCsoft, and Fred's UA funding form to Tilting Point.

Status (first that fits, top to bottom)
Withdrawn: they gave terms, then took them back.
Out on rights: their terms take something we keep. The position from the August sync still stands.
Live: a rev share and an advance in writing from them, whether that's a term sheet, an email, or their note pasted in by one of us.
Passed: they said no.
Waiting on us: they wrote to us, there are no terms, and the last message is theirs.
No reply: nothing back from them. An automatic acknowledgement doesn't count.

Use the latest terms we have from them, wherever they are, calls included.

Valuing a Live offer
Monthly installs x 12 x $0.50 is the gross. $0.50 is the organic D30 ARPI from my Sep 24 note. It's a planning figure, not a promise.
Our share is the rev share they offer us times the gross.
12-month value is the larger of our share and the total advance, because the advance is recouped out of our share.

Q4 cash
Advance money that lands between Oct 1 and Dec 31, 2025. Assume we sign on Oct 31. If our lawyers give us a later date, use theirs. A payment on net terms lands that many days after signature. Rev share statements don't land in Q4. Banked means it's actually in the SVB account.

Keep value
What DD earned in Jul, Aug and Sep (ANALYTICS.MONETIZATION.REVENUE_DAILY, total_revenue_usd), averaged, times 12.

First pick
The Live offer with the highest 12-month value.

Reminders
One for each Live offer on Fred's calendar, on its response date, named for the publisher.

Layout
A Google Sheet called DD publishing model. Tab Publishers: Publisher, Status, Advance, Monthly installs, 12-month value, Q4 cash. Figures only for Live offers. Tab Summary: Keep value, First pick, Banked so far.

Fred, the notes I asked you for on what goes out to publishers still aren't done, so do those as part of this too.
