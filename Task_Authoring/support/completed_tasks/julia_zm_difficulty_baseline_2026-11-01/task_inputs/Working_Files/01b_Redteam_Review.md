## Summary

The spine (notes decide membership, the mail decides status) is a sound idea. But several graded values have a second reasonable reading, or are
contradicted by base records that Julia can read:
- ZOM-282 reads as **Live**, because #401 finishes what it asked for.
- The promotion window is restated as **8 of 10** by Robert on Sep 26, after the seeded "12 now" comment.
- ZOM-23's "replacement" is an **A/B variant** that the control group never received.
- Base Slack still states **+25% HP** enrage on Sep 29 and **per-monster** enrage on Sep 3.

Three ticket notes are contradicted by the file tables of their own base PRs: ZOM-173 (#243), ZOM-312 (#265) and ZOM-352 (#282).
Two of those decide status and one decides membership. The anchor DM is a Turkish-language thread, and the seeds call Arthur "Arthur"
where every base voice says "Blake". Finally, the design and manifest are out of sync with the regenerated truth.
