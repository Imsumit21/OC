# Task business functions

Classify the work by its business purpose. These six existing categories and
target shares are task-distribution guidance, not additional persona roles or
per-task scoring thresholds.

| Business function | Target share | Typical work |
|---|---:|---|
| Engineering & Live-Ops | 25% | Engineering delivery, releases, incidents and live game operations. |
| Product & Design | 20% | Product decisions, gameplay/design requirements, art and prototype evaluation. |
| Growth / UA / Marketing | 15% | Acquisition, campaigns, marketing assets and vendor performance. |
| Founders / Exec / Strategy | 15% | Strategic decisions, pitches, priorities and executive coordination. |
| Finance / Legal / HR / Ops | 15% | Financial reconciliation, contracts, staffing and company operations. |
| Analytics & Data | 10% | Data pipelines, measurement, analytical reconciliation and reporting. |

Use the assigned function when supplied; do not invent an assignment. The
authorized roster remains exactly the 17 people in
`Evals/references/persona/roster.json`. A finance task must fit an authorized
person's actual responsibilities; it does not create a CFO persona.

Ground work, relationships and entities in supplied assignment context and the
selected local archive. Verify each requested action against the bundled tool catalog
and available run evidence, if a check requires observed behavior. This category guide does not declare tools, access rules
or current company facts. Role coherence, diversity and complexity are scored
under Eval 1 and `scoring.json`, not by enforcing distribution percentages.
